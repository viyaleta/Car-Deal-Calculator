Car Deal Calculator

See whether trading in or selling your car is the better deal, at each dealer, after tax and financing.

  1. Pick your state to fill in the sales tax rate.
  2. Start at the top of the tree with your current car: what you still owe and the best cash offer you've gotten for it.
  3. Fill in the dealer's deal and the trade-in they're offering. Add more dealers to compare. Every path is calculated, and the cheapest is highlighted.

Rates are state-level only, last checked November 2026, and may be out of date. They don't include county or city taxes. Look up your actual rate with your state's DMV or tax department and enter it above.

Net cost = price − discount − rebate + fees + sales tax + interest − what you get for your car + what you still owe on it. Trade in: the dealer pays off your loan, and any shortfall is added to the new loan. In most states the trade-in value also lowers the taxable price. Sell for cash: your loan is paid from the sale, and anything left goes toward the new car as extra down payment. There's no tax credit. Estimates only.
